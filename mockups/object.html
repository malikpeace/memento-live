The Memento itself, 15 forms

The card is one answer, not the only one. These are fifteen other things the Memento could be, all carrying the same living colour so evolution still works exactly the same way. Rough on purpose: shapes and finishes to react to, not final art. Real versions of the organic ones would need drawn or 3D assets rather than CSS.

01The card
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. What you have today. A flat pane, glass finish, burn border.
Good. Reads as a keepsake and an ID at once, holds text well, and shares perfectly as a story image.
Bad. Everyone's looks the same, and a rectangle is the most generic object a phone can show.

02The coin
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A struck medallion with a milled metal edge, the colour living in the face.
Good. Coins already mean earned, carried and kept. It is the most instantly understood object here and it stacks well if you ever mint one per goal.
Bad. Metal edges are close to the award and badge look Memento avoids, and a circle holds far less text than a card.

03The orb
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A glass sphere with the colour suspended inside it.
Good. Depth is the whole point. Colour moving inside a sphere reads as alive in a way a flat pane cannot, and it rotates beautifully on tilt.
Bad. No flat face means no text, no date, no name. It also needs real rendering to not look like a CSS ball.

04The monolith
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A tall standing slab, lit down one edge.
Good. The most severe and the most adult. It suits a memorial reading of the word better than anything else here, and it is trivially easy to render well.
Bad. Cold. It gives you nothing warm to feel about, and a narrow shape wastes most of a phone screen.

05The stone
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A worn pebble, matte, with the colour sitting under the surface.
Good. The only one that could visibly wear smooth over months, which is a real evolution story no other shape has. Feels like something you carry.
Bad. Irregular shapes are hard to keep consistent, and a dull rock is the easiest object here to make look cheap.

06The photograph
FridayDay 11
Mar 14 – Jul 24

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. Your own photo in a print frame, with the living colour washed over it as it evolves.
Good. The only form that is personal from the first second, because the content is yours. It also connects the Memento directly to the evidence wall.
Bad. Every user's looks completely different, so Memento loses the single recognisable object. Bad photos make bad Mementos.

07The vial
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A sealed glass vial that fills as you get closer to the goal. Right now it is 42% full.
Good. The only form where progress is the shape itself, not a colour change. You can read your position in one glance from across the room.
Bad. Needs a measurable goal to fill against, and an almost-empty vial after two weeks is genuinely demoralising.

08The band
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A closed ring with the colour living in the band and nothing in the middle.
Good. A closed loop is exactly what a streak is, and an incomplete ring is an obvious, wordless way to show a broken one.
Bad. Apple owns closing rings so completely that it will read as a reference no matter what you do to it.

09The shard
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A cut crystal with hard facets catching light differently on each face.
Good. Sharp, which is the design law made literal. New facets could appear as you go, giving evolution real geometry rather than just more colour.
Bad. Closest thing here to a game asset. Done badly it reads as a loot drop rather than a keepsake.

10The bloom
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. Your rose, abstracted. Petals open one at a time as the weeks pass.
Good. The only form with an obvious growth arc built in, and a bloom that closes when you stop is the clearest cooling signal of any shape here.
Bad. Living things in habit apps are extremely well-worn ground, and a flower is the hardest thing in this set to render without looking soft or twee.

11The ember
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A held flame, brightest at the base, that dims when you go quiet.
Good. Nothing communicates keep it lit faster. The cooling behaviour you already want is native to the object rather than bolted on.
Bad. Streak flames are the single most copied idea in this category. Also warm by nature, which fights the cool palette everywhere else.

12The point of light
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. No object at all. A single point of light that grows brighter and throws longer rays.
Good. Ties straight to the Neutron Star you already built, needs no new art, and it is the most restrained thing you could possibly put on the home.
Bad. Nothing to own or collect. It is a state, not a thing, and it will not survive being shared as an image.

13The pin
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A hard enamel pin: rounded square, raised metal border, deep colour inside.
Good. Small, collectible and immediately legible at widget and app-icon size, which none of the tall shapes manage.
Bad. Pins imply a set, and a set implies collecting many, which is a different product from having one thing you care about.

14The ingot
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A poured bar with a brushed face and a bevelled top.
Good. Weight and accumulation. Bars stack, so a hundred days could literally become a pile of them, which no other form does as well.
Bad. Reads as money and hustle, which is a narrower story than Memento is telling.

15The lantern
FridayDay 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

What it is. A frame holding a light: hard structure outside, living colour inside.
Good. Splits the object into a fixed part and a changing part, which is the cleanest way to sell skins later. The frame is the product, the light is you.
Bad. Two visual systems at once, so it is the most likely of the fifteen to end up looking busy.
