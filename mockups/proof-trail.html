Twenty answers to "show me the path I have been on." Same data every time: 11 day streak, 31 moves, goal set in March, four days missed. They differ in what they make you feel, not in styling.

What it is. A vertical timeline down the left edge, newest first, one line per day.
Good. Cheapest to build and it already matches the 7-day page, so the app gains a memory without a new visual language. Missed days sit in it as dimmed dots instead of vanishing.

19What you have stacked

What it is. Every finished day as a physical block, piled up, misses in grey at the bottom.
Good. Weight is the point. Thirty-one blocks feel like something you built, and the pile grows visibly week to week.
Bad. It says nothing about what you did, only how often. Past a few hundred days the pile stops being readable.
Seen in Brick and block metaphors in savings apps, Duolingo's XP piles.
