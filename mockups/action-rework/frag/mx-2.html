Built for the goals where the size of the day IS an amount of time (deep work, practice, training): the block is drawn to scale against the next two hours from right now, so 90 minutes literally looks like 90 minutes (the shortest block keeps a small floor so it stays readable). Every length is the plan’s own estimate, and the column is a ruler, not your calendar.
