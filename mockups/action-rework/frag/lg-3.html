The math

Why this plan

The struggle you named is diet, so that is where it aims. You cannot force an outcome, only supply inputs: hold the daily number and the goal arrives on schedule. The first weeks are the worst of it, habits move before the scale does, so the log is where progress shows first.

The plate

Build a meal plan of 2,000 to 2,500 calories and 200 to 250g of protein you can comfortably keep.

A 40 minute walk after dinnerit sits exactly where you said the day breaks
Log every meal in MyFitnessPalthe log shows progress before the scale does

How it runs

Every day. one screen: the walk, the log, a hold. Each morning. the scale number, typed at your own ritual. First look. two weeks. If it stalls. the plan changes, the goal doesn’t.

The deal

Your job is the acts. Memento’s job is to hold the math, ask the questions, and never let a day quietly disappear. Fair?
