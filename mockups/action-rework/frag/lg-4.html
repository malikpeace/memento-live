The logic: asked and answered

The brief as the questions the person would actually ask, in order, each with a short answer. Skim the bold questions and you have the summary; read the answers and you have the whole logic. Weight persona, same content.

“Lose 50lbs in 6 months so I look good for summer.”

How big is this, really?

You typed 6’1, 300lbs, and 50lbs in 180 days. A pound is roughly 3,500 calories: 175,000 total, about 1,000 a day. Big, and it is arithmetic.

So what do I actually eat?

At your size, maintenance is likely around 3,500 calories (an estimate your morning scale will correct). Eat around 2,500 a day and the goal follows, down to the laws of physics.

Why is the first move a meal plan and not a diet?

You cannot force an outcome, only supply inputs, and the struggle you named is diet. So the first input builds the one thing that makes 2,500 livable for 180 days: a meal plan of 2,000 to 2,500 calories, 200 to 250g of protein, yours. The walk after dinner and the meal log protect it.

What does a normal day look like?

One screen: the walk, the log, a hold. Each morning you type the scale number at your own ritual. First look at the math again: two weeks.

What if it stops working?

The plan changes, the goal doesn’t. Your job is the acts. Memento’s job is to hold the math, ask the questions, and never let a day quietly disappear. Fair?
