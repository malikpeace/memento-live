Stretch the number

The number is the control: you pinch it open or squeeze it shut and it physically grows on the screen, so a heavy day is literally larger than a light one before you read a single word. On the desktop the same stretch runs sideways, scrolling pulls the day out into a band that reaches the far edge of the frame at 20.
