Questions you might have

Why a meal plan and not a diet?

A diet is someone else’s. The first input builds the one thing that makes 2,500 livable for 180 days, yours: food you would actually keep eating. The walk and the log protect it.

What does a normal day look like?

One screen: the walk, the log, a hold. Each morning you type the scale number at your own ritual. First look at the math again: two weeks.

What if it stops working?

The plan changes, the goal doesn’t. Your job is the acts. Memento’s job is to hold the math, ask the questions, and never let a day quietly disappear.
