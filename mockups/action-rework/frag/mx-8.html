Three ways to say today

No number to set at all. Three real sentences of the same day, and at rest each one shows only its name and its opening line, so the screen is three short options and not three paragraphs. Tap one and it alone opens: the price, the consequence, and the sentence that becomes the task word for word. Choosing is a tap, committing is a hold.
