The whole logic, if you want it:

Losing weight comes down to math, and the struggle you named is diet, so that is where the plan aims. You typed 6’1, 300lbs. A pound is roughly 3,500 calories, so 50lbs is 175,000 calories over 180 days, about 1,000 a day. At your size maintenance is likely around 3,500 (an estimate your morning scale will correct), which lands the daily number at 2,500 eaten.

You cannot force an outcome. You can only supply inputs. Hold the daily number and the goal arrives on schedule, down to the laws of physics, and the first weeks are the worst of it: habits move before the scale does, so the log is where progress shows first.

So the first action builds the one thing that makes the number livable for 180 days: a meal plan of 2,000 to 2,500 calories, 200 to 250g of protein, that you can comfortably keep. The walk after dinner and the meal log protect it. Your job is the acts. Memento holds the math, asks the questions, and never lets a day quietly disappear.
