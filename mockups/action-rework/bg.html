The room: ten backgrounds

One screen, ten rooms. Every phone carries the identical statement and hold bar from the settled flow, so the only variable is what the black is made of. Ink only, never a hue. Where there is motion it is slower than attention on purpose: a background that can be caught moving is decoration, and decoration is banned. All ten freeze completely under reduced motion.

1Breath

Near-black with one barely-there pool of light behind the sentence, swelling and settling on an eleven second rhythm.

2Dust

A handful of faint ink specks drifting on paths so slow the room is never twice the same and never once in motion.

3Horizon

One thin line of light low on the screen, fading out before either edge. Nothing moves, and the sentence has a ground to stand on.

4Grain

No lines at all, a whisper of film grain, so the black reads as a material the screen is made of rather than an absence.

5Rules

The grid with its verticals taken out: wide-spaced ruled lines only, a dark notebook waiting for one line of writing.

6Contours

A few faint topographic curves in one corner, drawn once and never animated. Terrain, not pattern.

7Tide

The bottom fifth is a hair lighter than the rest. It rises while the hold is pressed and falls back on release.

8Hour

The black itself shifts by real time of day, deepest before dawn, a few values lifted by late afternoon. Scrub a day to see it.

9Wake

Nothing at rest. Touch leaves a slow-fading trace of light behind it, and the room forgets in about two seconds.

10Threads

Three long vertical hairlines at unrelated offsets. One of them travels about a pixel a minute and is only ever found somewhere else.
