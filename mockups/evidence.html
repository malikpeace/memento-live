12Slots you unlock

14 of 30

A slot opens every day you finish.

The last row opens at day 30. Nothing you can buy fills those.

What it is. A grid where future slots are visibly locked and open as you finish days.
Good. Creates real anticipation, and the empty slots are a goal in themselves. Nothing you can buy fills them.
Bad. Gamified in a way Memento mostly avoids, and locked boxes can feel like a nag rather than an invitation.
Seen in Apple Fitness awards, Duolingo's chest, collectible album pages.
