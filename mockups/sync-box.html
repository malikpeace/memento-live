The sync box, redesigned

Twenty-three candidates for the three swipeable faces under the Memento. Ten were designed per face by two designers working opposite briefs, then an adversarial critic fixed or killed each one. Seven were killed as filler or near-duplicates.

Every variant uses only data the app already stores. The test each one had to pass: what visibly changes here between day 2 and day 60. A card that shows state teaches you its rule in four viewings and is dead by week two. A card that shows change stays worth a glance.

Prototypes, not shipped. The Memento above each card is a stand-in for the real one.
