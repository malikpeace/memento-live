The sync box

Forty-five candidates for the three swipeable faces under the Memento. Thirty were designed conventionally, then fifteen more taking real risks. Every one has honest pros and cons under it, written by a critic told to be harsh.

Every variant uses only data the app already stores. The test each had to pass: what visibly changes here between day 2 and day 60. A card that shows state teaches you its rule in about four viewings and then returns nothing. A card that shows change stays worth a glance.

Prototypes, not shipped. The Memento above each card is a stand-in.
