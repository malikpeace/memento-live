The rules this follows

  • Nothing repeats page 1. No goal sentence, no date restated except where it IS the standing (the wall).
  • Four elements maximum, and the maintain shape drops to three. Anti-vision is the only dimmed text.
  • The standing slot swaps per goal shape: number goals get the distance, dated goals get the wall, no-finish-line goals get nothing, the why leads instead.
  • Update sits inside the standing card, only where a number exists to update.
  • "Change your Neutron Star" is deliberately quiet, present on every shape, routes to refine/redo.
