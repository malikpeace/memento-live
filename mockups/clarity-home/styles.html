Clarity page 2, four languages, six shapes

Same information in every phone: the standing (or the why leading when no honest number exists), the why, the anti-vision, the change button. The toggle switches the goal shape everywhere at once; the four phones never change WHAT they say, only HOW it looks. Style 1 is the pick from the last round, kept as the baseline.

1 · Cards

The current pick. Glass panels in the sync-box language, soft radii, one idea per card.

2 · Industrial

The action-module world: graph paper, monospace readouts, a ticked rail, hairline rules instead of boxes. Instrument, not furniture.

3 · Editorial

No containers at all. Giant type, thin rules, whitespace. The information IS the interface.

4 · Bands

Full-bleed tonal bands, edge to edge, no floating cards. The anti-vision band drops to near-black.
