Clarity notes, the full flow

The life of a note in the real module's language (nebula, #8be9fa, solid white pill). No why-gate: after Clarity they just spent the whole session on why. Instead, an optional message to future-you. Writing is full-screen with optional memory-jogs; on finish they add a simple tag. The stack lives in Clarity, Add sits at the top, and the latest note glances onto the Memento card.

1 · An optional message to future-you

Right after Clarity. Not "why this matters", they just did that. A reminder they can return to when it gets hard. Fully skippable.

Before we continue.

Leave a message for future you. As you start and keep making progress toward your goals, it's very easy to forget why you started. But you started for a reason. I highly recommend writing a personal note to remind yourself that you have a dream, so when your future self inevitably hits a lower point, they can stay in the fight.
