What each section is doing

  • The climb replaces the flat bar. Distance becomes something you look UP at: the star at the summit, fifths as posts (the same fifths logic from the celebrate rigs), your white dot and number where you actually are, the lit rail behind you. Day 1 = dot at the bottom, rail unlit, still a real place.
  • The two clocks is the honest tension: distance covered vs time spent, both plain facts, no verdict attached. It says what a pace line would say without saying it.
  • The fork is the why made spatial: the future they chose lit, the default dimmed, both "still true from here." Stakes, not decoration.
  • The plan is your "9 of 14 blockers": the built plan's state as tick marks plus the single step they are standing on.
  • The promise is the new piece: written in their OWN words when the star is forged (or added here later), shown with their name, the date made, and how long it has been held. Tap to reread full-screen or renew it. No seals, no stamps, their words carry it.

Flags for your call
