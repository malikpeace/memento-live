Clarity page 2, the final five

Every one of the ten was rendered and judged against four questions: is this too much information, is it clear, would a human like it, and is it actually a clean professional interface or decoration pretending to be one. Five survived, each in a genuinely different language: a stack, a climb, a grid, a letter, an orbit. Slop trims applied where noted.

The five that died, and why. The lit road: clean but its slider styling reads as an input you might fat-finger, and the climb covers the same idea better. The fork: the plan got tangled inside the future card and it needed two invented filler lines to hold together. The ledger: the quietest, but its giant-number wrap broke and its labels confused the date of the goal with the date of the promise. The bands: good, but it is the stack in a different coat, and the stack is more native. The waypoints: attaching your anti-vision to the 40,000 post implies meaning that is not there, decoration pretending to be information, which is exactly the AI-slop tell this pass existed to kill.
