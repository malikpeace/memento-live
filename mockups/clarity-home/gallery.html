Clarity, after the star

What tapping Clarity becomes once someone has finished it. Today it shows the star, the sentence, the timeframe, and one CTA. But the synthesis already generated their why, their future, their anti-vision, their identity, and the tension underneath, all sitting in state, never shown again. These are five directions for what this surface could be. Same star hero in all five; they differ in what lives beneath it.

Persona everywhere: Maya, "Grow the channel to 100,000 subscribers", 6 months, 41 days in, currently at 12,400.

The star stays the hero. Scroll and their own material unfolds beneath it: the why, the future, the anti-vision, the identity. Nothing new is generated; the hidden synthesis finally gets shown, in their words.

The job: the goal's shrine. One place where the whole picture of what they chose lives, reachable any time.

3 · The Fire

A re-entry built to re-light, not inform. Two beats: the future they chose, the default they refused, then out. Closer to a ceremony than a page, using only motion, scale, and darkness.

The job: the thing you tap on a low day. Ninety seconds, leave burning.

Why this direction

  • The one thing the app can't do yet: give you the feeling back on demand.
  • Future vs anti-vision side by side is the strongest contrast the synthesis owns.
  • In the live build the two visions fade in as beats, star breathing between them. No new colors, no new fonts, per the special-moments law.

Risk

  • If it plays identically every time it goes stale; beats should rotate anchor lines.
  • It answers "remind me why" but not "show me my goal's details."

Pure existing tokens. The dimmed anti-vision line stays at .5 alpha deliberately, it is the one place dim text IS the meaning.

5 · The Check

A quiet recommitment surface. Once in a while, or whenever they open it, Clarity asks one honest question: is this still the one? Recommit with a hold, or refine it.

The job: keep the star TRUE. A goal that drifted from the person is the silent killer of everything downstream.

Why this direction

  • The hold-to-recommit is a real physical moment, same gesture language as ignition.
  • "Something changed" routes into refine, which already exists.
  • Recommit events are honest fuel for the drift-intercept notifications later.

Risk

  • As the ONLY thing shown it wastes most visits; nobody needs the question daily. Likely a beat inside another direction, not the whole surface.

One primary action per surface. The hold reuses ignition's gesture, nothing new invented.

These combine. The likely real answer is a spine plus organs: one direction as the main surface, pieces of the others folded in (the Check as an occasional beat, the Distance card inside the Full Picture, the Fire as a button). Pick the spine and which organs, and the next round mocks the combined screen properly.
