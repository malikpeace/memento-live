What actually leaves the app onto someone's story. Each phone here is the exported image itself, not a screen inside Memento. This is the only free growth loop Memento has.

01Just the object
MEMENTO

Day 11

What it is. The card alone on black. No stats, no words.
Good. Works as a flex precisely because it does not explain itself, which makes people ask what the app is.
Bad. Nobody who sees it learns anything, so it converts curiosity slowly and only among people who already ask.
Seen in Spotify's now-playing share, Apple Music covers, collectible posts.

02The number
MEMENTO
11

days without breaking

Longest run I have ever had at anything.

What it is. One huge tabular number and one plain line under it.
Good. Cheapest to build, reads instantly at story scale, and survives being screenshotted small.
Bad. Typography-only cards all look alike. Two users' posts would be nearly identical.
Seen in Spotify Wrapped's stat cards, Strava's PR cards, Duolingo milestones.

Goal: 100 paying users. At 1.

What it is. A plain statement of your record, including the days you missed.
Good. Sharing the misses is what makes it read as real rather than a highlight reel. Honesty is what gets reposted.
Bad. It is a lot of small text for a story, and some people will not want their misses public at all.
Seen in Strava's honest stats, Whoop's weekly report, BeReal's late stamp.

04The shape of it
MEMENTO

Four months. Two collapses. Still here.

Every bar is a week I showed up.

What it is. Your four-month pattern as bars, dip included.
Good. Shares the shape of the effort rather than one number, and it is personal enough that no two look alike.
Bad. Needs months of history before it means anything, so new users cannot share it.
Seen in GitHub graph screenshots, Strava's Year in Sport, Wrapped's charts.
