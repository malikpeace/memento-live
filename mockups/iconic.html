Ten more, built for everyone

The first ten all lean on one founder chasing a number. These ten each carry a DIFFERENT kind of promise from the taxonomy: a rule held (sobriety), a frequency (the gym), an open goal (the writing), a deadline event (the bar exam), a daily practice (meditation), a family rule (dinner by six), and one for people with many goals at once (the shelf). None of them needs a countable target to work.
