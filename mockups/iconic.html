Built for everyone

The survivors of the universal round: the Vow (a rule held), the Comeback (returning as the skill), the Firsts (progress as moments), the Count (days left, spent honestly) and the Month (one verdict per month). None needs a countable target to work.

The Vow

A written promise, kept. The rule they set for themselves is the headline, their own reason sits under it, and one number says how long it has held. For rule goals there is no distance to show, the holding is everything.

Best for
Anyone holding a line: sobriety, no gambling, off the phone by ten, any promise where success is a day that looks like the day before.
The share
The whole vow card: the rule, their reason in their own words, and the unbroken count, which posts like a chip earned rather than a chart.
On day 900
On day 30 it reads as a beginning; on day 900 the same layout reads as a monument, because the only thing that changed is the number, and that is the point of a vow.
