Five alarms

The sound at the end of a deep work block. Same brief for all five: finishing an hour of real work should feel like being told, not startled. No shrill tops, no repeating buzz, nothing that sounds like a phone alarm.

Every one is synthesised live on this page, exactly the way the app makes its own sounds, so what you hear is what would ship.

These repeat, the way an alarm does: it rings, waits, and rings again until it is answered. Tap once to start, tap again to stop. Judge them on the third and fourth ring, not the first, and listen with music playing too. The question is not which is prettiest, it is which one you would still want to hear on the four hundredth time.
