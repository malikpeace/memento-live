One universal record for every kind of goal. The bloom is the same for everyone: one seed per day you showed up, in a golden ratio spiral, growing into a flower over time. The goal shape only tunes the line under it and one contextual number. Below the hero, every real statistic the record can honestly produce. Switch the day count, the goal shape, and the accent to stress every layout.

Numbers-first heroes — the data is the hero

Maybe the top of the Consistency page should not be a graphic at all. Twenty numbers-first treatments, five each of a stat sheet, dashboard tiles, a headline sentence, and a scoreboard. Numbers read from day one, which is exactly where a picture struggles. Drag the slider to scrub 0 to 365. Honest, no flattery.

Twenty-four hero visuals — replacements for the bloom
