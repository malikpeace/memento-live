The mission, five ways

The same three facts every time: 9 of 100 are in, today's move, 17 of 24 days with a run of 3. Pick the one that feels least like a widget.

1. The sentence

No labels at all. The mission read out loud as one human sentence, the three pillars living inside it as colour.

You want 100 paying users, and 9 are in. Today you message ten people who fit the product. You have shown up 17 of the 24 days.
