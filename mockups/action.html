Action, 10 ways

What you see the second Action opens. Same move every time: "Talk to three users about why they signed up." Every one has a focus option, placed differently on purpose.

01One move, nothing else

Friday

Talk to three users about why they signed up

Maximum restraint. The move is the whole screen. Nothing to read, nothing to decide. Closest to the design law, but gives you no sense of context.

02Timer is the screen

Talk to three users about why they signed up

18:42

25 minutes. Phone face down.

Focus is not a button, it is the default state. Opening Action means you are starting now. Strongest anti-procrastination design; wrong if most moves are not timed work.

03On the card

Today

Talk to three users about why they signed up

Day 11

The move lives on the Memento itself, so doing it visibly feeds the object. Ties Action to the thing you are building. Risk: text on a bright card is a contrast fight.

04One big, one small

Day 11

Talk to three users about why they signed up

About 40 minutes.

If today falls apart

Send one of them a single message.

A built-in floor. On bad days the streak survives without lying. This is the single highest-leverage retention idea on this page.

05Move and reason

Talk to three users about why they signed up

You have 8 users and no idea which part they actually needed. You cannot get to 100 guessing.

Two lines total: what, and why it is the move today. The why is where the AI earns its cost. Kills the "why am I doing this" drop-off.
