Reflect, 5 ways

The writing surface. What matters here is how little friction there is between opening it and having written something, because almost nobody journals on purpose.

01Blank page

Friday night. What is on your mind?

Marcus stayed for the streak, not the card. That changes what I should be building

What it is. A quiet prompt, then a blank page.
Good. No formatting, no title, no mood picker. The least standing between you and the first word.
Bad. A blank page is exactly what stops most people writing. There is no help if you do not know what to say.
Seen in Bear, iA Writer, Apple Notes.

02Asked about today

You said Priya never replied. Does that change the plan?

Type anything.

What it is. The question comes from what you actually did today.
Good. Much harder to ignore than a generic prompt, and the clearest place the AI justifies its cost.
Bad. One AI call per day that has to be specific and correct, or the question feels worse than no question.
Seen in Sunsama's prompts, Reflectly's adaptive questions, Stoic.

03Three slots

Friday

Worked

Calling instead of emailing. Two answered in an hour.

Did not

Trying to do it at 4pm when I am fried.

Tomorrow

—

What it is. Three short answers: worked, did not, tomorrow.
Good. Finishable in under a minute, which is the whole game, and it produces structured text the AI can actually use.
Bad. The same three questions every night gets boring fast, and it can produce answers on autopilot.
Seen in Sunsama's shutdown, agile retros, the Five Minute Journal.

04Say it out loud

Hold and talk. It writes it down.

0:24

What it is. Hold and talk. It writes it down for you.
Good. Removes typing, which is the actual reason journaling dies on a phone. People say things they would never type.
Bad. Needs transcription, which costs money and gets names wrong. Also useless in public.
Seen in Day One's audio entries, Otter, Voice Memos transcription.

05Letter to yourself

Opens in 30 days. You will have forgotten you wrote it.

If you are reading this and you stopped, it was because you started building again instead of talking to people. Go call someone

Sealed until Aug 23

What it is. A message to yourself that unseals in 30 days.
Good. Gives Memento a reason to notify you that has nothing to do with nagging, and the arrival moment is the most shareable thing here.
Bad. The payoff is a month away, so it is unprovable at launch, and a letter arriving after someone quit could sting.
Seen in FutureMe, Apple Journal's reflections, time-capsule features.
