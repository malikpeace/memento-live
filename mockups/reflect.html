Reflect, 5 ways

The writing surface. What matters here is how little friction there is between opening it and having written something, because almost nobody journals on purpose.

01Blank page

Friday night. What is on your mind?

Marcus stayed for the streak, not the card. That changes what I should be building

The prompt sits above in low contrast and then gets out of the way. No formatting, no title, no mood picker. Closest to what Reflect already is, just quieter.

02Asked about today

You said Priya never replied. Does that change the plan?

Type anything.

The question comes from what you actually did today, not a generic prompt. Much harder to ignore, and it is the clearest place the AI earns its keep.

03Three slots

Friday

Worked

Calling instead of emailing. Two answered in an hour.

Did not

Trying to do it at 4pm when I am fried.

Tomorrow

—

Three short answers instead of one blank page. Finishable in under a minute and produces structured text the AI can actually use to change tomorrow's move.

04Say it out loud

Hold and talk. It writes it down.

0:24

Removes the typing barrier entirely, which is the real reason journaling dies. People will say things out loud they would never type, and you get transcript text either way.

05Letter to yourself

Opens in 30 days. You will have forgotten you wrote it.

If you are reading this and you stopped, it was because you started building again instead of talking to people. Go call someone

Sealed until Aug 23

A message that arrives from your past self a month later. Gives Memento a reason to notify you that has nothing to do with nagging, and it is the most shareable thing in this set.
