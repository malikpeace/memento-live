Fifteen ways to look back

Prototypes for the surface that shows everything a person has done. The current one is a stack of grey month rows, which has no shape, no meaning and no scale reference, and at three years it becomes thirty-six identical rows.

Three directions, five each. Every one uses only data the app already stores, and every one has honest pros and cons written by a critic told to be harsh. Each frame scrolls on its own.

Sample data throughout is a user fifteen months in: 462 marks, 318 active days, a 103 day best run, one goal replaced along the way.
