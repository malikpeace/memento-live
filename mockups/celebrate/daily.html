The daily reward, 8 directions

The everyday hit, fired the moment someone completes their action. Not a milestone: no confetti, no colour flood, green rather than their Memento colour, quiet enough to see every day for a year. It counts actions completed, not calendar days, so a person who shows up three times a week is never told they missed four.

Everything below is live. The slider at the top drives all 8 screens at once, so you can see any of them at 1 action or 365. The whole screen is green: this is the page that replaces the plain green flash the moment you hold to confirm an action. Tap a phone to play its motion, then tap again to dismiss.
