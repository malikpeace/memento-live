Fuck with the values

Every phone below is driven by the same logic file the app will use (chooser.js), not by hand-placed pixels. Change the numbers and watch the layout solve itself. The line under each phone tells you whether a celebration would actually FIRE at that state, because part of being dynamic is knowing when to stay silent. Fourteen panels cover every approved screen that HAS variables; the ones missing here (qd-3's "under 200" line, ms-1's flood, ms-4's decision, op-3's proof count, the card's M pop) are words and motion around these same numbers, so tuning the panels tunes them too.

Quantity: the marker posts (qu-1) / the slider down (qd-1)

One renderer, both directions. Goal below start = the slider down; above = the posts. Marks come from chooser.milestones(), never hand-picked.

Quantity up, no target: four figures (qu-2)

No goal number, so the win is the digit itself. Fires only the day the number grows a digit.

No-target records: the record board (qu-5) / the record book (qd-2)

The only thing to beat is themselves. A new best must clear the old one by 2% (or a whole unit), at most once a week.

Deadlines: the days you will not need (qu-3) / early by (ms-3)

A deadline goal finished early. The celebration is the plain subtraction.

Frequency: Total runs (fr-3)

The big number has to survive 5 and 5,000,000. The font fits itself; the ceremony fires on ladder rungs only.

Maintenance: the ledger of days (mt-1) / the first seven (mt-4)

The ladder is 7 / 30 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 365, then every 100. Day 6 and day 87 stay silent on purpose.

Maintenance: held to the date (mt-2)

A hold with a finish line. The goal closes the day the deadline passes with the line unbroken.

Maintenance: the hard ones (mt-3)

Only exists when hard days were REALLY logged (the log is offered on quit-a-habit goals only). The list windows to the last few; it can never overflow.

Milestone: the engine (ms-2)

A binary event with the quantity engine underneath. The counts are whatever they actually logged, never invented.

Your picks: the gap (rf-gap) / the fall (rf-fall)

Same three numbers, two tellings: the distance closing, or the number they carry dropping. Toggle to compare.

Open: then vs now (op-1)

Two of their own entries held apart. Fires first at day 30, then on the ladder, never a surprise year later.

Your picks: the record (rf-rec)

The grid is a WINDOW: always the last 12 weeks at most, older history becomes one quiet line. It can never grow off the screen.

Frequency: week chips (fr-4)

Also windowed: the last 8 weeks as chips, everything older summarised. Tolerant by design, a missed week is a dimmer chip, not a broken streak.

Open: day N, the goal and the why (op-4)

Words + counts only, since an open goal has no metric. Singular and plural both have to read like a human wrote them.
