The count (Total runs)

The big number has to survive 5 and 5,000,000. The font fits itself; the ceremony fires on ladder rungs only.

Days held (maintenance)

The ladder is 7 / 30 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 365, then every 100. Day 6 and day 87 stay silent on purpose.

The record (twelve weeks of days)

The grid is a WINDOW: always the last 12 weeks at most, older history becomes one quiet line. It can never grow off the screen.

Week chips (frequency)

Also windowed: the last 8 weeks as chips, everything older summarised. Tolerant by design, a missed week is a dimmer chip, not a broken streak.

Open receipts (day N, the goal and the why)

Words + counts only, since an open goal has no metric. Singular and plural both have to read like a human wrote them.
