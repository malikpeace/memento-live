Plan & billing

The You panel with a real billing destination. Everything here is wired to things that already exist: the plan and renewal date come from the verified access receipt (currentAccess().validUntil, fetched today, shown nowhere), Manage plan opens the Polar portal (openPortal()), the guarantee row opens the existing refund dialog. New backend needed: none. The Account drawer keeps sign out, sync and delete; billing moves out of it into its own section. Four states, left to right: a monthly subscriber, a Founder, a subscriber whose renewal is off, and a paid user who signed out on a new device.
