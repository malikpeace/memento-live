Your Color, on the real Memento

Every card below is built with the production card recipe (the exact liquid layer, blob colors, positions, 46px blur, screen blending and clip rules from daycard-living.css). The house lights are the four you named: cyan (Clarity), platinum (Action), green (Consistency), periwinkle (the accent). The idea: the first three lights are MEANING and never change. The periwinkle slot is the ACCENT slot, and that is where a person's own color lives.
