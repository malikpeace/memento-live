How the leash works: the name hashes to a point inside FIVE approved families only (glacier blues, deep teals, spring greens, ember reds, violet-free twilight indigos). Saturation and brightness are clamped to the app's glass logic. No random uglies, no purple, no gold, ever. Two people can share a family but almost never the exact degree, the color name shows the uniqueness ("Glacier 207°").
