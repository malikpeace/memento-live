Privacy

Effective July 28, 2026

Memento is built local-first. Without an account, your goals, answers, notes, reflections, and images live on your device. If you create an account, your supported Memento data can be securely synced so you can restore it on another device.

What stays on your device

Your Clarity answers, goal, daily moves, notes, reflections, streaks, and check-ins are stored in your browser's local storage first. Vision-board and journal images never sync. No account is required for the free Clarity experience. An account is required to sync between devices or buy paid access. If you use Memento without an account, clearing your browser data erases your local work, so use the backup button in your Profile first.

What leaves your device, and why

  • AI features. When you use an AI feature (like Clarity discovery or the coach), the text needed for that feature is sent through our server to Anthropic, the company behind the Claude models, to generate the response. Anthropic does not train its models on this data. We do not store your AI conversations on our servers.
  • Anonymous usage signals. We record which screens get used (events like "app opened" or "action completed") tied to a random device id, never to your name, email, or anything you write. This tells us what to improve. No third-party analytics, no advertising trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • Reminders, if you turn them on. To send push notifications we store your device's push address, timezone offset, the name of your current daily move, and whether today is done. Turning notifications off in your system settings expires the subscription and we delete the record.
  • Sync, if you create an account. Signing in with an email code or Google mirrors supported Memento data to our database so you can pick it up on another device. It is protected so only your account can read it. Images still never sync.
  • Payments. Polar processes purchases as our merchant of record. We never receive or store your full card number. To decide what you can access, Memento stores limited billing records such as your Memento user id, billing environment, plan and product identifiers, Polar customer or subscription identifiers, access status, paid-through date, verification times, and any Founder-place reservation. These records are not your card details.

What we never do

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not share anything with data brokers. Your payment buys access to Memento; your personal data is not the product.

Deleting your data

Reset Everything in your Profile wipes only the current device copy. Delete Account permanently removes your Memento account and associated server records after you confirm your identity again. Once the server confirms deletion, Memento also clears the current device.

Before deleting the Memento account, we ask Polar to cancel active subscriptions and anonymize the connected billing customer. Polar may retain transaction records it is legally required to keep for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute handling, and other legal obligations.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app, not bury it here.
