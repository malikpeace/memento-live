Card light

Six ways light can live on the Memento. Tilt your phone, or drag a card with your finger. Same card, same skin. Only the light changes.

A

Studio specular

One soft key light. The highlight follows the tilt, the shadow leans away. The classic "held under a lamp" look.

B

Brushed sheen

A fine metal grain with one bright band that sweeps as you tilt. The platinum feel, on every skin.

C

Rim light

The light lives on the edges. Tilt back and the top edge catches it, tilt forward and the bottom does. The glow behind breathes slowly.

D

Depth parallax

No highlight at all. Three layers of the material slide at different speeds, so the card reads as deep instead of shiny.

E

Liquid light

Light moves inside the material on its own, slow as water. A small bright point still answers your tilt.

F

Foil shift

A foil sheen that rotates around the card as you tilt. Kept strictly silver, never rainbow.

Every option carries its exact recipe in this page's source. The pick gets ported verbatim onto the real card, on every skin, with the tilt driven by device motion the way this page does it.
