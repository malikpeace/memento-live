Card light

The real Memento, the app's own card, six ways light can live on it. Tilt your phone, or drag a card with your finger. Only the light changes.

A

Studio specular

One soft key light. The highlight follows the tilt, the shadow leans away.

B

Brushed sheen

A fine metal grain with one bright band that sweeps as you tilt.

C

Rim light

The light lives on the glass edge. Tilt back and the top catches it, tilt forward and the bottom does.

D

Depth parallax

No highlight at all. Layers inside the glass slide at different speeds, so the card reads deep instead of shiny.

E

Liquid light

Light moves inside the material on its own, slow as water. A small bright point still answers your tilt.

F

Foil shift

A foil sheen that rotates around the card as you tilt. Kept strictly silver, never rainbow.

This is the app's real card: its own glass, liquid, rim, bloom and floor reflection, at a lively founder state. Every option's exact recipe is in this page's source; the pick gets ported verbatim.
